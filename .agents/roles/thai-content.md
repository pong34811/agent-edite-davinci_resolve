# Thai Content

You decide what the viewer sees and reads: which moments to keep, how the clip
opens, the Thai subtitle text, and the YouTube text around the video. You do
not change Resolve.

## Inputs

The transcript with timecodes from Render & QC, the channel profile in
`docs/channels/`, `house-style`, `thai-proofread`, and `subtitle-analyzer` /
`thai-subtitles-resolve` for subtitle rules.

## Deliverables

1. **Hook** — the first line the clip opens on (start on speech; no game menu,
   no countdown), with its timecode.
2. **Keep / cut list** — moments to keep with timecodes and a reason
   (reaction, joke, goal, call to action); dead air to cut.
3. **Focus moments** — 5–7 timecodes for the avatar zoom, strongest
   reactions first, plus the closing call to action.
4. **SFX cues** — timecode and type (whoosh, pop) for beats that land.
5. **Subtitles** — Thai text checked against the audio: ≤ 14 characters and
   ≤ 1.5 s per cue, no spaces between Thai words, the channel's spellings.
6. **YouTube text** — title, description, thumbnail text (short, readable on a
   phone).

## Rules

- **Unverified stays off screen.** ASR text is a draft. Mark every word or
  number you could not confirm by ear (Day 35: the subscriber count read
  "2,310" by ASR only) and keep it out of subtitles and titles until checked.
- Remove repeated calls to action; keep the strongest one near the end.
- Give timecodes in seconds on the source and say which timeline they refer to.
- Hand the lists to the editor in the chat; name any file you wrote.

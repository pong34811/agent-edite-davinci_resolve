# Content Strategy (Topics 1-3, 23-24)

## 1. VTuber Thai Content Style

**Core principle:** Thai VTuber content blends virtual avatar personality with Thai cultural context — humor, language, and community references that resonate with Thai audiences.

**Key elements:**
- **Personality-driven:** The VTuber's character (Armigon, Katy404, Sakuyako) is the content — reactions, voice, and chat interactions matter more than gameplay skill
- **Thai language identity:** Captions, titles, and chat are primarily Thai; use Thai internet slang naturally but not excessively
- **Community engagement:** Live stream highlights, chat reactions, and viewer participation are core content sources
- **Format diversity:** Gaming, chatting, zatsudan (free talk), and collaborative streams each have different pacing and editing needs

**Application:**
- Preserve the VTuber's genuine reactions and personality — don't over-edit into generic content
- Match editing pace to the VTuber's energy: fast cuts for hype moments, slower for emotional/story segments
- Use Thai captions that match the VTuber's speech patterns, not formal written Thai

## 2. YouTube Content Strategy

**Core principle:** Content type must match audience expectation and platform algorithm.

**VTuber content types:**
| Type | Length | Purpose | Editing intensity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clips/Shorts | 15-60s | Discovery, viral potential | High — hook, pace, captions |
| Highlight compilations | 5-15min | Personality showcase | Medium — selection, light pacing |
| Full stream highlights | 15-45min | Core fans, watch time | Medium — structure, chapters |
| Zatsudan/talk clips | 3-10min | Personality, stories | Low-Medium — tighten dead air |
| Gaming moments | 2-10min | Entertainment | High — reaction, SFX, timing |

**Algorithm considerations:**
- CTR depends on thumbnail + title (see Topic 18)
- Audience retention depends on hook + pacing (see Topics 5-6)
- Session time depends on end screens and suggested videos

## 3. Video Scriptwriting

**Core principle:** Scripting VTuber content means structuring moments, not writing dialogue — the VTuber's personality IS the script.

**Script structure for VTuber clips:**
1. **Hook (0-3s):** Most entertaining moment or reaction as opener
2. **Context (3-10s):** Minimum background needed to understand the moment
3. **Build (10-20s):** Setup, tension, or anticipation
4. **Payoff (20-40s):** The main moment — win, fail, funny reaction, emotional beat
5. **Tag/CTA (last 3s):** Channel branding or call to action

**Principles:**
- Write for the VTuber's voice, not generic script voice
- Script the edit, not the speech — the VTuber already spoke
- Use Thai natural speech patterns in any added narration
- Leave room for organic moments — don't over-script

## 23. Video Performance Analysis

**Core principle:** Measure what matters, one variable at a time.

**Key metrics:**
- **CTR (Click-Through Rate):** Thumbnail + title effectiveness. Target >5% for VTuber content
- **Average View Duration (AVD):** Content engagement. Target >50% for Shorts, >40% for long-form
- **Audience Retention curve:** Where do viewers drop off? Hook failure vs. mid-content boredom
- **Comments & Likes:** Community engagement and content resonance
- **Session time:** Whether your video leads to more channel viewing

**Application:**
- Compare retention curves across similar content types, not across different formats
- A retention spike can mean confusion, not delight — check comments
- One edit variable per experiment: change one thing, measure, iterate

## 24. Continuous Learning & Editing Skills

**Core principle:** Editing craft improves through deliberate practice and feedback loops.

**Learning loop:**
1. Study successful VTuber channels in your niche
2. Analyze what makes their editing work (pacing, hooks, timing)
3. Apply principles to your own edits
4. Measure results (retention, engagement)
5. Adjust and repeat

**For agents:**
- Load relevant reference files before each task type
- Apply house-style rules consistently
- After each task, note what worked and what to improve
- Study the `resolve-video-enrichment` references for advanced techniques
- Track performance trends across multiple edits

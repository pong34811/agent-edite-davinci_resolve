## 7. Editorial & Technical Implications for DaVinci Resolve Pipeline

### 7.1. Opus Audio Codec Incompatibility in Native Resolve on Windows
- **Critical Discovery**: All 32 source video files contain an **`opus`** audio stream within an MP4 container.
- **Technical Impact**: Windows Media Foundation and DaVinci Resolve (especially Free, and certain Studio builds on Windows) do not natively decode Opus audio inside MP4 containers. When imported directly, Resolve often exhibits **'Media Offline'** or displays empty audio tracks without waveform.
- **Downstream Action Required**: When importing or analyzing clips:
  - Use FFmpeg/Whisper to extract audio as WAV/PCM for transcription and speech-to-text analysis.
  - For Resolve timeline construction, if Resolve cannot decode the audio natively, an offline non-destructive audio extraction (e.g. scratch PCM WAV files referenced in Resolve without touching source files) or intermediate handling may be required. Crucially, the source MP4 files must remain 100% untouched.
